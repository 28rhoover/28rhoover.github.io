About the Developer!

I am a passionate web developer with a love for creating games revolving around my original characters! I love building lore for characters, and may eventually create a master list of all of the possible information there is to know about my OCs. I have minimal experience in RPGMaker (particularly RPGMaker MZ), but I perform best when utilizing HTML, CSS, and Javacript. While I myself am not a proficient programming prodigy (woo, get a load of that alliteration), I like to feel proud of my current standing, whilst simultaneously not becoming complacent.
